# The four scenes

Each scene is written once per tool, the way that tool's docs teach:

- ultrafast: a JSON document, generated by `ultrafast/build.py`
- Remotion: React components in `remotion/src`
- HyperFrames: HTML, CSS and a paused GSAP timeline in `hyperframes/<scene>/index.html`

All are 10 s at 30 fps (300 frames). All use the same font files (Geist and Geist Mono,
SIL OFL, `assets/fonts`). Times are in seconds. "Out-cubic" means 1 − (1 − p)³:
ultrafast `out_cubic`, Remotion `Easing.out(Easing.cubic)`, GSAP `power2.out`. Text is
placed by the top-left of its line box (CSS `line-height: normal`; ultrafast `valign: top`)
unless noted.

## (a) title: 1080×1920 social title card

- Background: vertical linear gradient #1e1b4b (top) to #be185d (bottom), sRGB interpolation.
- Sun: circle, 640 px, #fbbf24. Centre drifts linearly from (760, 560) to (700, 640) over
  0–10 s; scales 0 → 1 over 0–0.9 s, out-cubic.
- Tile: rounded square 520 px, radius 72, #22d3ee, centre y 1700. Slides in from
  x −360 to 250 over 0.3–1.2 s, out-cubic; rotates 8° → 20° over 0–10 s, linear.
- Ring: circle outline 220 px, 10 px white, centre (250, 560). Opacity 0 → 0.9 and
  scale 0.6 → 1 over 1.4–2.0 s, out-cubic.
- Copy, left edge x = 120:
  - bar: 14 px tall, radius 7, white, centre y 1000; width 0 → 160 from the left, 1.0–1.5 s.
  - kicker "NEW DROP · 2026": Geist Mono 500, 40 px, tracking 6 px, white 80 %, top 1030;
    fades in and rises 30 px over 0.8–1.3 s.
  - "Make it": Geist 800, 190 px, white, top 1090; fades in and rises 80 px, 1.0–1.7 s.
  - "move.": Geist 800, 190 px, #fbbf24, top 1280; same, 1.15–1.85 s.
  - "Ten seconds. One render.": Geist 500, 52 px, white 85 %, top 1500; rises 20 px, 2.0–2.6 s.
  - The whole copy block fades out and rises 60 px over 8.8–9.6 s (in-cubic).

## (d) effects: 1920×1080 "GPU looks" (not in the headline; see README)

- Background #07060d.
- Three circles, 560 px, each blurred with a Gaussian of σ = 80 px (CSS `blur(80px)`;
  ultrafast `blur` radius 80): #7c3aed from (600, 420), #06b6d4 from (1340, 380),
  #f43f5e from (900, 800), each moving to (1000, 640), (980, 560), (720, 520) over
  0–5 s and back over 5–10 s, in-out-sine.
- "GLOW": Geist 900, 260 px, white, centred on (960, 540), with a white glow (CSS
  `text-shadow: 0 0 48px`; ultrafast `glow` radius 24). Fades in and scales 0.9 → 1 over
  0.5–1.5 s.
- "GPU EFFECTS TEST": Geist Mono 500, 32 px, tracking 8, #c4b5fd, centred, top 720,
  fades in over 1.2–1.8 s.
- Whip: a white pill 360×28 at y 860 crossing the frame from x −300 to 2220 over
  1.0–1.6 s and back over 6.0–6.6 s (in-out-cubic), with motion blur at a 180° shutter:
  ultrafast format `motion_blur` (8 samples, up to 32 on fast moves); Remotion
  `<CameraMotionBlur>` (its default 10 samples) around the whip; HyperFrames
  `data-motion-blur="on"` with `data-motion-blur-shutter="180"` (its default: at least
  32 samples per mover).
- Vignette, then film grain over everything: ultrafast `vignette` and `grain` (0.07);
  the browser tools use a radial-gradient vignette and HyperFrames' registry
  `grain-overlay` block (a 256 px SVG-noise tile shifted ten times per 0.5 s); Remotion
  shows the same tile with the same offsets.

The looks in (d) are close, not identical: CSS blurs in sRGB while ultrafast blurs in
linear light, and the grain is a different noise. `frames/effects.jpg` shows all three.
